How two-camera mode works

The entry camera records entry when it recognizes a registered member who is not already inside. The exit camera records exit only when an open visit exists. Frame display and recognition run separately to keep the preview responsive. Test in your actual doorway before relying on logs.

Recommended mapping

Keep the built-in laptop camera as one role and your external 4K webcam as the other. OpenCV identifies USB cameras by index (0, 1, 2…), not by a guaranteed hardware name. Use Test source to confirm which physical camera each index represents.
